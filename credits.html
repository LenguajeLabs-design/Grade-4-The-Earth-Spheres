EARTH EXPLORERS / ABOUT THIS READER

Sources & image credits

These four readers adapt the English texts and Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese translations prepared for the Grade 4 Earth’s Spheres unit.

Illustrations

The images are illustrations from the original Earth Explorers readers. The realistic scenes are photorealistic illustrations, rather than photographs documenting particular places or events. Diagrams are simplified models. Images are stored with the website so reading does not depend on a separate image service.

Science references

  • NASA: Connect the Spheres
  • UCAR: Earth as a System
  • USGS Water Science School
  • USGS Ecosystems Mission Area
  • National Park Service: Geology
  • NOAA / National Weather Service: Lightning Safety

Language and listening

English stays visible. Choose a home language to see its translation after each paragraph. Use the Listen buttons for an individual paragraph or a whole section. Included synthetic narration covers English (Samantha), Japanese (Kyoko), Korean (Yuna) and Chinese (Tingting). Whole-paragraph recordings are stored with the site and play at a gentle pace by default. Optional device voices depend on the browser and installed voices, and some may use an internet service. Written translations always remain available.

Japanese readings in parentheses support selected science terms. The translated texts have not had independent native-speaker review.

For teachers

The main texts use the short passages from the classroom readers. They have not been independently assigned a WIDA or Lexile level. The geosphere definition includes Earth’s rocks, land and interior, consistent with the section on molten rock. In this four-sphere model, ice is included in the hydrosphere; other resources may name the cryosphere separately.

The site does not ask students to sign in or submit personal information. Reading-language, speed, size and voice preferences are saved only in the browser. Hosting and device speech services operate under their own policies.
