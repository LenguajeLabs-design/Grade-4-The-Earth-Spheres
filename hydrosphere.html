The Hydrosphere

Water Everywhere on Earth

01 / DISCOVER

What Is the Hydrosphere?

The hydrosphere is all the water on Earth. Water can be liquid, solid ice, or water vapor in the air.

We can find water in oceans, lakes, rivers, streams, and even under the ground.

AMAZING FACT

Most of Earth's surface is covered by water.

02 / EXPLORE

Water Can Move

Rivers and streams move water across the land. Water can flow fast or slow.

Moving water can shape the land. It can carry soil and tiny pieces of rock.

LOOK + THINK

What clues show that the water is moving?

Ice Is Water, Too

Glaciers are large bodies of ice. Snow and ice are part of the hydrosphere.

Frozen water can stay on mountains and in very cold places for a long time.

SPHERE CONNECTION

A glacier may sit on land in the geosphere, but the ice itself is part of the hydrosphere.

03 / CONNECT

Why Is Water Important?

People, animals, and plants all need water to live. Water helps keep living things alive.

Water also moves heat around Earth and helps shape weather.

THE HYDROSPHERE HELPS LIFE

Another amazing job: Water can change form. It can freeze, melt, and evaporate.

Water Is Always Traveling

Water can rise into the air, form clouds, and fall as rain or snow.

Then water can flow through rivers and streams back to lakes and oceans.

WATER CYCLE · DIAGRAM KEY

  • Evaporation: water rises.

  • Clouds: form in the air.

  • Rain falls.

  • Snow and ice.

  • River flows to the ocean.

04 / YOUR MISSION

Earth Is Connected!

Rain falls from the atmosphere. Water flows over land in the geosphere.

It helps plants and animals in the biosphere. One kind of water can connect many parts of Earth.

FOUR SPHERES

Water is part of many Earth systems at the same time.
