The Geosphere

The Ground Beneath Our Feet

01 / DISCOVER

What Is the Geosphere?

The geosphere includes Earth's rocks, soil, mountains, and land.

The geosphere also includes the rock deep inside Earth.

AMAZING FACT

Some mountains are very high, but they are all made of rock from the geosphere.

02 / EXPLORE

Rocks and Soil

Soil is part of the geosphere. Many plants grow in soil, and many animals live in or on it.

Rock can be big like a cliff or small like a pebble.

LOOK + THINK

What do you notice in the soil layers and stones?

Inside Earth Is Very Hot

Magma is melted rock beneath Earth's surface. When magma reaches the surface, it is called lava.

Volcanoes are part of the geosphere. During an eruption, hot lava can flow over the land.

SPHERE CONNECTION

A volcano is geosphere, but the smoke and ash also affect the atmosphere.

03 / CONNECT

Rocks Can Change

Weathering breaks rock into smaller pieces. Wind, water, ice, and temperature changes can cause weathering.

Erosion moves the small pieces to a new place.

THE GEOSPHERE CHANGES

Another amazing job: Moving water can cut through rock and make canyons over a long time.

Rock to Sediment

A rock can crack and break. The small pieces are called sediment.

Rain and rivers can carry sediment away and build new land somewhere else.

ROCK CHANGE · DIAGRAM KEY

  • Weathering: rain, wind and ice.

  • Rock: solid Earth material.

  • Sediment: small pieces of rock.

  • Erosion: moving water carries sediment away.

04 / YOUR MISSION

Earth Is Connected!

The geosphere holds soil for plants in the biosphere. Rivers in the hydrosphere can move rock and soil.

Wind and rain from the atmosphere can also change the land.

FOUR SPHERES

Land does not stay the same. Other spheres can change it.

5 words to remember

soil

the top layer of land where plants grow

magma

melted rock under Earth's surface

weathering

breaking rock into smaller pieces

erosion

moving rock or soil to a new place
